Welcome to ANITS Information Portal

Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology and Sciences is a premier engineering college located in Visakhapatnam. The college is well known for its quality education and encourages innovation, research, and technical excellence. Students learn scientific concepts such as H2O and mathematical formulas like x2 + y2 = z2.

Popular Engineering Departments

  • Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
  • Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)
  • Information Technology (IT)
  • Mechanical Engineering (ME)

ANITS Admission Process

  1. Qualify in the AP EAPCET (EAMCET) state-level entrance exam.
  2. Participate in the online web counseling process.
  3. Select ANITS as your preferred college option.
  4. Report to the campus with original academic certificates.
