vision

A college vision statement is a clear, forward-looking goal that describes what the institution wants to achieve in the future. It focuses on shaping student success, providing quality education, driving research, and serving the community

Mission

A college mission statement is a short declaration that explains the core purpose, values, and goals of an educational institution. It outlines how the college plans to serve its students, support learning, and impact the wider community.
